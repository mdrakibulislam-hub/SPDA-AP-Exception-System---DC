DataCrunch design exercise

Who checks and approves a bill?

The AP specialist finds out why a bill is stuck. The people who can give the OK depend on how much the bill is for and whether there’s a purchase order.

Approval hierarchy

As a bill crosses each amount limit, another person must say yes. Amounts are in US dollars or the equivalent in another currency.

  • Under $5,000

    • Budget owner

  • $5,000 to $50,000

    • Budget owner
    • Department head

  • Over $50,000

    • Budget owner
    • Department head
    • Finance controller

No purchase order? The budget owner must always approve. Any additional approvals for that amount still apply.

The brief names who must approve and leaves the order open. Original brief, page 2

What each person can do

AP specialist
Bill checker
Checks why the bill is stuck. They compare what was billed, what the company ordered, and what arrived. They can gather evidence and send the bill to the right approvers.
Budget owner
Manager in charge of the money
Gives the OK to spend money from their budget. This is the only approval listed for bills under $5,000.
Department head
Person who leads the team
Adds their approval for bills of $5,000 or more. For example, if Operations bought the parts, the Operations Director might be the department head.
Finance controller
Finance lead
Adds their approval for bills over $50,000. Each of SPDA’s three legal entities has a controller. They enforce finance rules, review the record of changes and decisions, and track what’s holding up the end-of-month accounts.
Supplier
Company that sent the bill
Can answer questions about their bill. If your design includes a supplier view, it can show where their own bill stands and when they can expect payment.

Example from the brief

Vantage Industrial Components · $96,400

Vantage emailed new bank details. Because the bill is over $50,000, the budget owner, department head, and finance controller all have to approve.

The bank change has its own check. One person calls a supplier contact SPDA already knows. A second person approves the change before anyone pays using those details.

What you get to decide

  • Who can fix details that the AI read wrong and who can see those changes. Every change or decision must record who did it, when, and why.
  • How approval requests are sent and what happens when the assigned approver is away.
  • Which roles make the bank verification call and approve the change. The brief requires two different people.
